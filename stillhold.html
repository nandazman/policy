Privacy Policy — Stillhold

Effective date: 2026-07-19
App: Stillhold — Apnea Trainer (package com.nandazman.stillhold)
Developer contact: nandafazman@gmail.com

Summary

Stillhold is a breath-hold (static apnea) interval trainer. Everything you create in it — your session history, notes, ratings and settings — stays on your device. There is no account, no login, and no server operated by us. The free version shows ads through Google AdMob, which is the only component that collects anything; a one-time purchase removes it.

Data we collect

None ourselves. We operate no servers and receive nothing from the app. There is no analytics SDK and no crash reporting.

Data processed on your device (and never sent anywhere)

DataWhyLeaves device?
Session history — date, mode, rounds, total time, longest holdSo you can see your training over timeNo
Notes and RPE ratings you typeYour own annotations on a sessionNo
Settings — tables, prep time, sound and motion preferencesTo restore the app as you left itNo

This is held in a private database inside the app's sandbox and is removed when you uninstall the app. The app can export a copy to a file you pick; once you have chosen where to save it, that file is outside the app's control and yours to manage.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows an interstitial ad after a finished session. To serve it, Google AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, coarse device and network information, and ad interaction data. This processing is Google's, under Google's privacy policy.

Where consent is required — the EEA, the UK and other regions — the app shows Google's consent form on first launch, and your choice there controls whether ads are personalised. You can reopen that form later from Settings → ad privacy, and you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android's own settings.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

The one-time "remove ads" purchase is handled entirely inside Google Play. The app never sees or stores your payment details — Play tells it only whether the purchase exists. Buying it stops the ads, and with them AdMob's collection.

Permissions

  • Foreground service, wake lock (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SPECIAL_USE, WAKE_LOCK) — so a session keeps counting and speaking cues with the screen off and the phone in your pocket.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — to show the running session and its controls.
  • Vibrate (VIBRATE) — phase cues you can feel.
  • Internet, network state (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) — to fetch ads and verify the purchase. With ads removed, the app makes no network requests of its own.
  • Advertising ID (AD_ID) — required by AdMob to serve ads.

Children

Stillhold is not directed at children and is not intended for anyone under 18. Breath-hold training carries a real risk of blackout and should never be practised unsupervised, or in or near water without a trained partner watching.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it holds. There is no server-side copy for us to delete. If you have questions, email the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with a new app release and the effective date above will change.
