Privacy Policy — Silentry

Effective date: 2026-06-21
App: Silentry (package com.silentry.app)
Developer contact: nandazmann@gmail.com

Summary

Silentry is an offline call blocker. It does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app does not even hold the Android INTERNET permission, so it is technically incapable of sending your information anywhere. Everything happens on your device.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect or transmit any personal or device data. There are no analytics, no advertising SDKs, no crash reporting, and no servers operated by us.

Data processed on your device (and never sent anywhere)

To screen incoming calls, the app processes the following locally on your device only:

DataWhyLeaves device?
Incoming phone numberTo check it against your allow-list / block-listNo
Caller display nameTo read carrier spam flags (e.g. "Spam", "Potential")No
Your ContactsSo people you know are never blockedNo — read on-device, never copied or uploaded
Block-list / allow-list / blocked-call historyStored in a local database you controlNo

All of this is stored in a private SQLite database inside the app's sandbox and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Permissions

  • Read Contacts (READ_CONTACTS) — to allow calls from people in your address book. Contacts are read on-device for matching only and are never stored or transmitted by Silentry.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — to notify you when a spam call was blocked.
  • Ignore Battery Optimizations (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS) — so the system does not stop call screening in the background.
  • Call Screening Role (ROLE_CALL_SCREENING) — the Android role required to screen and reject calls.

Children

Silentry is suitable for all ages and does not collect data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with a new app release and the effective date above will change.
