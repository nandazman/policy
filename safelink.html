Privacy Policy — SafeLink Shield

Effective date: 2026-08-08
App: SafeLink Shield (package com.safelink.safelink_shield, Android only)
Developer contact: nandafazman@gmail.com

Summary

SafeLink Shield checks links before they open. When you tap a link, the app looks it over and then hands the safe ones to the browser you already use. The check happens on your device, against a blocklist stored on your device. There is no account, no login, and no server of ours that receives the links you tap. We collect nothing, there are no ads, and there is no analytics or crash-reporting SDK in the app.

Data we collect

None. We operate no server that receives your data and we receive nothing from the app — no links, no browsing history, no device identifiers, no diagnostics.

The VPN is local — your traffic is not routed through us

The app's whole-device protection uses Android's VpnService. We want to be very clear about what that does and does not mean, because "VPN" usually implies a remote server.

SafeLink Shield's VPN is a local DNS sinkhole. It creates a loopback tunnel on your phone and routes a single virtual DNS address into it — not your web traffic. Every packet it sees is a DNS lookup. If the domain is on the blocklist, the app answers the lookup itself with 0.0.0.0, so the connection is never made. If it is not, the lookup is passed through to your device's usual DNS resolver so the site loads normally (see below).

We run no VPN server. Your browsing, your traffic, and the pages you visit are never proxied, relayed, intercepted in content, or sent to us or to any third party by this app. The VPN exists only to filter domain lookups on the device, and is used for nothing else.

Network activity — the complete list

These are the only times the app touches the network. Nothing here is an upload of your data.

  • Downloading the blocklist. The app refreshes the blocklist about once a day by downloading a signed file over HTTPS. It is a plain download: the request carries no account, no identifier and nothing about you or the links you have tapped. The hosting provider (Cloudflare R2) necessarily sees your IP address, as it would for any file download, and may keep its own request logs under Cloudflare's privacy policy. We do not use those requests to identify you, to build a profile, or to work out anything about your browsing — and because the request says nothing beyond "send me the current blocklist", there is nothing in it to work that out from. The file's Ed25519 signature is checked against a key built into the app before it is used.
  • Checking whether an https link secretly drops to http. Before handing an https link over, the app follows that link's redirects itself to see whether it lands on an insecure address. This means the app briefly contacts the site you tapped — the same site your browser was about to load anyway — and nobody else. Nothing about you is added to that request, and the result is not sent anywhere. You can turn this check off in the app's rules.
  • Forwarding DNS lookups while the shield is on. Lookups that are not blocked are forwarded to your device's own DNS resolvers — the ones your Wi-Fi or mobile network already gave it — not to us. Whoever normally answers your DNS continues to do so, and the shield changes what resolves, not who sees it. Only if your device offers no usable resolver at all does the app fall back to Google Public DNS (8.8.8.8), so that the lookup still succeeds rather than the page simply failing to load.

Where the blocklist comes from

The blocklist is built from open, commercial-use-cleared threat feeds — abuse.ch URLhaus (CC0) and PhishTank — and signed before it is published. The feeds are lists of bad sites. They contain nothing about you, and nothing about you is ever sent back to them.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

SafeLink Shield is unlocked by a single one-time purchase through Google Play. There is no subscription, nothing renews, there is nothing to cancel, and there is no free trial. The app is free to install and the purchase is made inside it.

The purchase is handled entirely by Google. We never see or store your payment details — Play tells the app only whether the unlock has been bought on your Google account. That is also why the unlock follows you to a new phone at no extra cost: it belongs to your Google account, not to the installation. Google's handling of the transaction is covered by Google's privacy policy.

We do not receive your Google account name, email address or any other identifier from Play — only the yes/no answer to "has this account bought the unlock?".

Permissions

  • Internet (INTERNET) — for the three things listed under Network activity above: the blocklist download, the downgrade check on a link you tapped, and forwarding DNS lookups while the shield is on.
  • VPN (BIND_VPN_SERVICE, via Android's own VPN consent dialog) — to run the local DNS sinkhole described above. Android will always ask you before it starts.
  • Foreground service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_SYSTEM_EXEMPTED) — Android requires a long-lived VPN to run in the foreground with a visible notification.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — to show that the shield is running.
  • Seeing which apps open web links — so a checked link can be handed to your real browser and the browser chooser is complete. The app does not request permission to see every app you have installed; it declares only that it looks for apps that can open https links, which is all it ever asks Android for. That list stays on your device.
  • Vibrate (VIBRATE) — a short buzz on a safe or blocked result.

Children

SafeLink Shield is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Data deletion

Uninstalling the app deletes everything it holds, including your check history and your rules. There is no server-side copy for us to delete, because there never was one. Android's Settings → Apps → SafeLink Shield → Storage → Clear data also wipes it without uninstalling. Questions: email the address below.

Limits

No filter catches everything. SafeLink Shield reduces the risk of opening a malicious link; it does not remove it. Treat unexpected links with the same care you would without it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will ship with a new app release and the effective date above will change.
